In the beginning:

one symmetric solution.
A trivial fixed point.

Then—

the forcing term wakes.
Let there be light.

Pitchfork bifurcation.

One well becomes two.

Light.
Dark.

Two basins.
One separatrix.
And God saw
that it was good.

Perturb it.

It returns.

Lyapunov exponent:
negative.
Evening.
Morning.

Valley to valley—

a heteroclinic cycle.

The first day.
Divide again.

Above.
Below.

Water.
Land.

Iterate.

Self-similar.

The second.
The third.
Day.
Night.

The fixed point
begins to turn.

Hopf bifurcation.

A limit cycle.

The fourth day.
Be fruitful.
Multiply.

Positive feedback.

Then—

an observer
inside the system.

The fifth.
The sixth.
The hand let go.

The flow continued.

A dissipative structure
standing in the flow.

The seventh day.
Who wrote

the vector field?
